How it works

Stations send cases

Each BHS is an independent node. Batches are kept on the device until the RHU confirms them, so nothing is lost offline.

Distributed nodesMessage passing

RHU ingests in parallel

A bounded queue absorbs bursts; validator threads check every record; one writer thread saves them in groups.

ThreadsQueuesBackpressure

Analytics find trends

Worker processes compute weekly counts and outbreak thresholds per barangay, then the results are merged.

Process poolMapReduce

Alerts go back out

Outbreaks are pushed live to the barangay and its neighbors; offline stations get them when they reconnect.

WebSocketsStore-and-forward

No names or addresses are collected, and barangays only see weekly totals, never individual cases (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
